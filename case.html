The AI-project case

Part of the assignment (A001): a written page and an oral presentation, in pairs

A firm considers investing in an AI system that takes over part of its tasks. Is the investment profitable, when should it start, and does it cut jobs? You answer with the tools of the course: free cash flow, NPV, IRR and sensitivity analysis.
